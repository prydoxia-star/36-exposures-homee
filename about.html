01 / WHERE IT BEGINS

Company History

36 EXPOSURES grew from a love of the slower, more intentional side of photography. In a world where images are captured and forgotten in seconds, we wanted to make room for the process: choosing a roll, waiting for the negatives, and seeing a photograph come to life. Based in Singapore, our independent studio brings film development, scanning, printing and analogue essentials together in one welcoming place.
